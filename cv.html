ABOUT & CV

Doga Cedden

Dr. rer. nat. · Molecular entomology & RNA biology

My research focuses on RNA interference and the molecular regulation of insect physiology. I combine experimental approaches with bioinformatics to investigate RNA function, plant–insect interactions, and seasonal dormancy.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Applied Entomology group at Justus Liebig University Giessen, funded through an independent DFG Walter Benjamin project. I also hold a research associate position in the Bioresources section at Fraunhofer IME.
