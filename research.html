Research areas

Experimental and computational approaches to RNA function, plant–insect interactions and seasonal dormancy.

01

RNA interference↗

Target-gene selection, dsRNA sequence design and the molecular effects of RNAi in insects.

02

Natural dsRNA↗

Naturally occurring double-stranded RNA and its potential contribution to crop defence.

03

Diapause & aestivation↗

04

Small RNAs↗
